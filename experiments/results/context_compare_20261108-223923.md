**Liability limits (gap):** For the debit transfers, Reg E says that if state law or an agreement with the institution imposes less liability than the section provides, the consumer's liability cannot exceed that lower amount [R-1005.6#2]. For the card purchases, Reg Z treats a charge not made by the consumer or someone with actual, implied, or apparent authority as a billing error [R-1026.13#1]. While a billing error is pending, the consumer need not pay the disputed amount, and the creditor may not try to collect it [R-1026.13#2].

**Deadlines, Reg Z (card):**

Credit card (Reg Z) and debit card (Reg E) have different limits. This assumes the $700 was on a credit card, which the request doesn't say.

**Credit card:** Liability for unauthorized use cannot exceed the lesser of $50 or the value obtained before notice to the issuer [R-1026.12#1]. Liability exists only if the card was accepted, the issuer gave adequate notice of the liability limit and how to report, and the issuer provided a means to identify the cardholder [R-1026.12#1]. A lesser limit under state law or agreement governs [R-1026.12#1]. Deadlines: a written billing error notice must be received within 60 days after the first statement showing the error was transmitted [R-1026.13#1]. The creditor must acknowledge within 30 days and resolve within 2 complete billing cycles, and in no event later than 90 days [R-1026.13#1].

**Debit card:** If the consumer notifies within two business days of learning of the loss or theft, liability is capped at the lesser of $50 or the unauthorized transfers before notice [R-1005.6#1]. Without timely notice, the cap is the lesser of $500 or a stated sum, which adds later transfers only if the institution shows they would not have occurred with timely notice [R-1005.6#1]. Unauthorized transfers shown on a periodic statement must be reported within 60 days of transmittal to avoid liability for later transfers [R-1005.6#1]. Time limits extend for extenuating circumstances [R-1005.6#1], and a lesser limit under state law or agreement applies [R-1005.6#2]. Liability also depends on the required disclosures having been given [R-1005.6#1].
